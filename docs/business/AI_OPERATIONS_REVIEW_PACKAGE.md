# AI Operations Review Package

Last updated: 2026-05-26

## Positioning

The AI Operations Review is the front-door diagnostic for Onyx AI Studio. It is not a chatbot consultation. It maps how a business currently handles calls, website visitors, lead intake, scheduling, documents, follow-up, and internal knowledge before recommending what AI should or should not do.

## Beachhead

For the next 30 days, the active beachhead is granite and countertop quote-intake systems. The proof path should point to the Countertop Estimate Tool and the operational problem of turning vague website interest into structured quote requests.

## Review Format

- Initial intake through the website review form.
- Each submission is reviewed within one business day.
- Follow-up by email or call after the submission is reviewed.
- Best-fit discovery call length: 30-45 minutes.
- Deliverable: a workflow map, first-system recommendation, source-material checklist, readiness notes, and first-system scope.
- Turnaround target: respond to qualified submissions within one business day.
- Privacy posture: the submission is used to evaluate fit and plan the next step; it is not sold or added to bulk outreach.

## What Onyx Reviews

- Current website and lead capture path.
- Call handling and missed-call risk.
- Quote or intake workflow.
- Scheduling and callback rules.
- Existing forms, FAQs, service pages, SOPs, and customer questions.
- CRM, inbox, spreadsheet, or other follow-up process.
- Sensitive topics, escalation rules, and data boundaries.

## Output

The review should produce one of these recommendations:

- Build a website quote or intake path first.
- Build reception and website assistant coverage first.
- Build an internal knowledge/data assistant first.
- Clean up source material, handoff rules, or lead tracking before AI goes live.
- Decline or pause if the requested automation would be unsafe or too vague.

## Fit And Not-Fit Handling

- Strong-fit projects move to a 30-45 minute review call or async workflow review.
- Not-fit or not-ready projects receive a practical cleanup/readiness next step instead of a forced build pitch.
- The recommendation should name what to build first, what not to automate yet, and which source materials or handoff rules are needed.

## Fit Criteria

Good fits have repeated calls, quote requests, lead capture gaps, scheduling friction, staff lookup problems, or messy but usable business records. Weak fits want a vague AI experiment, unsupported scraped data, staff replacement, or an assistant that makes promises the business cannot consistently keep.

## Pricing Relationship

The review is the sales diagnostic before implementation. Paid implementation remains separate from client-paid Retell, Supabase, hosting, email, SMS, calendar, or other platform usage.
