# Beachhead Decision

Last updated: 2026-05-26

## Decision

Onyx AI Studio will focus the next 30-day sales and proof sprint on granite and countertop quote-intake systems.

## Why This Lane

- Founder credibility is strongest around stone fabrication and countertop buying behavior.
- The Countertop Estimate Tool is already live proof.
- The operational problem is specific: buyers need guided quote context before the shop spends manual time.
- The offer maps cleanly to AI Operations Review, lead capture, follow-up, and structured business data.

## Primary Buyer Problem

Countertop shops often receive vague calls, showroom visits, or form submissions before the buyer has clarified material, square footage, edge details, sink cutouts, backsplash, islands, timeline, and budget expectations. A guided quote-intake system can collect better context earlier.

## Front-Door Offer

The CTA remains AI Operations Review. The beachhead framing should explain that Onyx will inspect the quote path and recommend the first system: estimator, intake flow, receptionist, website assistant, lead routing, or internal summary layer.
