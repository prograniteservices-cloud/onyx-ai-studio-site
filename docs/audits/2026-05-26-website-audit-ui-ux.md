# Website Audit: UI And UX

Date: 2026-05-26
Site: https://onyxaistudio.digital
Workflow: Website Audit
Agent role: UI/UX Auditor
Original score: 88/100
Roundtable-adjusted score: 86/100

## Summary

The user experience is now strong enough to support serious buyer evaluation. Mobile conversion access, the staged contact form, pricing clarity, founder trust signals, proof sections, and the AI Operations Review concept all improved.

After the roundtable, the score moves from 88 to 86 because the remaining gaps are business-conversion gaps, not cosmetic issues. The Review is the critical front-door offer, but it is not yet concrete enough on the live conversion surface.

## Strengths

- Mobile CTA visibility and header clarity are materially improved.
- The contact form is staged and asks for the right first-pass context.
- Status and loading states are present, and the form path feels more intentional.
- First-viewport credibility is stronger.
- Pricing is clearer and supports premium positioning.
- Trust hierarchy is better: founder identity, LinkedIn, demos, case studies, pricing, process proof, and Review explanation are all present.
- The brand feels more like a serious operational AI studio than a generic chatbot vendor.

## Weaknesses And Risks

- The AI Operations Review needs clearer buyer packaging on public pages: turnaround, deliverable format, free/paid/credited status, what happens after submission, privacy, and fit criteria.
- Trust is still mostly self-authored. The site needs stronger external proof, specific workflow artifacts, screenshots, and eventually testimonials or outcome data.
- The 20-demo portfolio proves range but can dilute the main sales story.
- Service pages remain dense and should be more scannable.
- Reveal behavior should be hardened so content never depends on fragile intersection timing.
- The Review CTA must feel like a specific diagnostic, not a nicer contact form.

## Priority Recommendations

1. Add a "What you get after the AI Operations Review" block on `/contact`, the homepage CTA area, and the Review insight.
2. Include format, timeline, deliverable, free/paid/credited model, fit criteria, and next step after submission.
3. Add outcome-led proof modules: countertop quote intake, before/after lead flow, sample Review output, sample lead record, and workflow map.
4. Refocus homepage and portfolio hierarchy around 3-5 business-relevant proof paths before the full demo library.
5. Add a privacy and data-handling trust block near the lead form.
6. Make service pages easier to scan with problem, system, output, and best-fit patterns.
7. Harden accessibility and interaction details: mobile menu keyboard flow, form status/focus behavior, and validation clarity.
8. Reduce nonessential homepage and services animation while preserving the premium operational feel.

## Evidence Gathered

- Reviewed live conversion surfaces, mobile path, form behavior, pricing, portfolio, case studies, and Review positioning.
- Compared UX findings with frontend performance, SEO/GEO, and business-management risks.

## Roundtable Position

The agent agreed that proof and productization are now the ceiling. The correction sprint fixed the obvious UX blockers, but a premium buyer still needs a concrete diagnostic offer and stronger outcome proof.

The agent cautioned against flattening the visual identity while reducing script cost. The aesthetic is an asset; the fix is to remove avoidable weight and friction.

Score adjusted from 88/100 to 86/100.
