# Website Audit: AI Agency Business Management

Date: 2026-05-26
Site: https://onyxaistudio.digital
Workflow: Website Audit
Agent role: AI Agency Business Management Auditor
Final score: 84/100

## Summary

Onyx AI Studio is credible enough to sell premium AI operations work from the site. The correction sprint closed several serious gaps: the Review CTA is clearer, the mobile path is stronger, the form is staged, leads persist to Supabase, pipeline fields exist, the granite/countertop beachhead is named, and the proof story is better aligned.

The site is not yet a premium AI operations sales machine. The remaining gap is the last 20 percent: public productization of the AI Operations Review, operator-visible lead pipeline management, stronger outcome proof, and one disciplined niche sales path.

## Strengths

- Clear strategic position: managed AI business operations, not generic chatbot work.
- The active beachhead is explicit: granite/countertop quote-intake systems.
- Pricing supports premium positioning, with setup and monthly management tiers that can sustain agency economics.
- Lead capture is real infrastructure: `/api/contact` validates staged required fields, saves to Supabase, then sends a Resend notification.
- The Supabase lead table includes lead score, priority, pipeline status, due dates, milestones, outcome, estimated value, review type, and notification status.
- The lead handling SOP states that every Review submission needs an owner, status, next action, and follow-up deadline.
- The Countertop Estimate Tool case study includes a clearer proof story and first-build recommendation.
- Live technical checks are healthy across sampled commercial routes.
- Search Console performance shows impressions even while sitemap indexing remains immature.

## Weaknesses And Risks

- The AI Operations Review is documented better than it is productized on the live conversion surface.
- The CRM is schema-ready, not operator-ready. There is no admin view, dashboard, report, due-date view, booking handoff, or weekly pipeline review surface.
- Proof is still capability-heavy. The Countertop page needs buyer-grade proof: before/after workflow, sample lead output, implementation timeline, expected ROI logic, screenshots, and eventually testimonials or outcome data.
- The portfolio can dilute the main offer when it leads with breadth before business outcomes.
- Delivery risk is under-documented for Retell, Supabase, assistants, guardrails, launch testing, privacy/retention, monitoring, and offboarding.
- Homepage performance remains a business concern because it affects buyer trust.
- Mobile menu keyboard behavior is improved but incomplete.
- Some project docs contain drift against the actual contact form requirements.

## Priority Recommendations

1. Add a public "What you get after the AI Operations Review" block on `/contact`, homepage CTA section, and the Review insight.
2. Build the smallest usable lead pipeline view: new leads, priority, status, next action due, notification status, source/campaign, contacted/booked/proposal/closed fields.
3. Create one flagship granite/countertop proof page or landing path around the Countertop Estimate Tool, expected lead record, quote-intake workflow, before/after intake map, and Review CTA.
4. Reduce homepage script pressure while preserving the visual identity.
5. Promote 3-5 proof paths before the 20-demo library: countertop quote intake, missed-call/reception workflow, internal knowledge assistant, messy data/VapeOS, and lead follow-up automation.
6. Add delivery SOPs for discovery, source intake, assistant rules, Retell setup, Supabase schema, testing, launch, monitoring, monthly review, privacy/retention, and offboarding.
7. Align outreach to the beachhead for the next 30 days.
8. Fix documentation drift so future operators do not treat optional qualifiers as required first-step fields.
9. Add Escape/focus-return behavior to the mobile menu.
10. Monitor Search Console for 7-14 days.

## Evidence Gathered

- Reviewed project state, business docs, audit docs, contact page/form/API, Supabase schema, pricing/about/portfolio/case-study surfaces, and centralized site data.
- Checked live commercial routes for status, H1, canonical, and indexability.
- Reviewed Search Console context and sitemap state.
- Incorporated frontend, UI/UX, and SEO/GEO audit findings.

## Roundtable Position

The agent agreed that basic credibility, crawlability, and conversion access are no longer the blockers. The current bottleneck is business-system maturity: a productized Review offer, proof hierarchy, authority/entity reinforcement, and a clearer path from visitor to qualified sales conversation.

The agent cautioned against generic authority building before sharpening the sales offer. External entity signals help, but buyers still need to know exactly what the Review produces, what happens next, and why Onyx is credible for their operational problem.

Score stayed at 84/100.
