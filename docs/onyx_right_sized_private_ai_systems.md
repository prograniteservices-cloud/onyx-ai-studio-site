# Onyx AI Studio — New Service Chapter: Right-Sized Private AI Systems

## Purpose

This document explains a new service direction to add to **onyxaistudio.digital**.

This is **not a full pivot**. Onyx AI Studio still offers broader AI implementation services, including websites, lead systems, workflow automation, AI phone/web assistants, document assistants, and business AI integrations.

This new chapter adds a more advanced service category:

> **Right-Sized Private AI Systems** — custom AI assistants and model deployments sized to the business task, from tiny local task models to Mac Studio / NVIDIA-backed private AI systems.

The site should present this as another major service option under Onyx AI Studio, offered when it fits the client’s needs.

---

## Core Positioning

Most businesses do not need “the biggest AI model.” They need the **smallest reliable AI system that gets the job done**.

Onyx AI Studio should position this service around practical deployment choices:

- Tiny task-specific assistants
- Fine-tuned or adapted small models
- Private document assistants
- Local AI systems on Mac mini / Mac Studio / Apple Silicon
- Larger NVIDIA workstation or server deployments
- Hybrid cloud/local AI systems when appropriate

The key idea:

> **Use the smallest reliable model and deployment setup for the business problem.**

This makes Onyx AI Studio different from generic AI automation agencies that simply connect everything to ChatGPT or a large cloud model.

## What This Service Includes

This service can include any combination of:

1. **Micro Assistant Builds**
   - Small, narrow AI assistants for specific repetitive tasks.
   - Can run locally, in-browser, on lightweight devices, on mobile-capable hardware, or through low-cost inference.
   - Best for tasks that are repetitive and clearly defined.

2. **Fine-Tuned or Adapted Small Models**
   - Small models specialized around a business’s data, examples, language, forms, products, and workflows.
   - Useful when a generic model is too broad, too costly, or too inconsistent.
   - Best for classification, extraction, routing, drafting, summarizing, and repetitive office decisions.

3. **Private Knowledge Assistants**
   - AI assistants connected to a company’s internal documents, SOPs, pricing, policies, FAQs, product catalogs, or estimates.
   - Usually built with RAG/document search.
   - Best for businesses with scattered internal knowledge.

4. **Local Office AI Systems**
   - Private assistant deployed on local business hardware.
   - Possible hardware: Mac mini, Mac Studio, existing office machine, mini PC, or RTX workstation depending on requirements.
   - Best for privacy-sensitive businesses or businesses that want less dependence on cloud APIs.

5. **Large Private AI Infrastructure**
   - Higher-end local AI systems using NVIDIA GPU workstations or servers.
   - Best for businesses needing larger models, more users, heavier workloads, multimodal AI, or private internal platforms.

6. **Hybrid AI Deployments**
   - Combines local models, small task models, cloud models, and automation tools.
   - Best when some work should stay private/local while harder tasks can route to stronger cloud models.

## Example Use Cases

Use examples that business owners can understand.

### Lead Intake Assistant

- Reads customer messages
- Extracts name, phone, location, job type, urgency, and budget clues
- Classifies the lead
- Drafts a reply
- Sends the lead to a CRM, spreadsheet, or email inbox

### Quote Helper

- Turns messy customer requests into structured quote notes
- Checks pricing rules or material categories
- Builds a quote checklist
- Drafts an estimate message

### Office Document Assistant

- Searches company policies, SOPs, service lists, warranty terms, and internal notes
- Answers staff questions based on company documents
- Reduces repeat questions and wasted search time

### Customer Reply Assistant

- Drafts replies in the company’s preferred tone
- Uses business-specific examples
- Keeps messaging consistent

### Field Tech Helper

- Gives step-by-step checklists
- Summarizes job notes
- Captures before/after notes
- Helps standardize service reports

### Local Private AI Box

- Runs a private assistant in the office
- Keeps more business data on owned hardware
- Can be paired with document search, workflows, and staff access

## Important Technical Positioning

Do not overpromise.

Small models are powerful when the task is narrow and well-defined, but they are not magic.

Use this message:

> Small models work best when the job is specific, the examples are clean, and the output can be tested. For broader reasoning or heavy workloads, we can route to larger local systems or cloud models.

The website should make Onyx AI Studio look practical, not hype-driven.

Avoid saying:

- “Tiny models can do everything.”
- “We replace ChatGPT completely.”
- “No cloud AI is ever needed.”
- “We train your AI from scratch.”
- “Perfectly private and perfect accuracy.”

Prefer saying:

- “Right-sized for the task.”
- “Local, cloud, or hybrid depending on the job.”
- “Small models for narrow workflows.”
- “Private document search and business-specific assistants.”
- “Designed to reduce cost, protect data, and automate repeat work.”

The site does not need a technical deep dive, but the service page should distinguish these clearly in simple language.

### Private Knowledge Base / RAG

Use when facts change or the business has documents that need to be searched.

Best for:

- SOPs
- PDFs
- policies
- pricing sheets
- warranty terms
- service catalogs
- internal FAQs

Simple explanation:

> We connect the assistant to your business documents so it can answer from your actual company knowledge.

### Fine-Tuned / Adapted Small Models

Use when the business has repeat examples and wants the AI to behave in a specific way.

Best for:

- lead classification
- customer reply style
- form extraction
- estimate routing
- job note summaries
- quote draft formatting
- repetitive admin decisions

Simple explanation:

> We can adapt smaller AI models around your business examples so they perform narrow tasks cheaply and consistently.

### Best System

The strongest setup often uses both:

> A small adapted model for behavior + a private knowledge base for facts + workflow tools to take action.

|---|---|

| Cloud / hybrid | When reliability, remote access, or stronger models matter more than full locality |

Important note for the site:

> Hardware is recommended based on the actual workload. Some businesses only need a simple assistant; others need a local server or GPU-backed deployment.
